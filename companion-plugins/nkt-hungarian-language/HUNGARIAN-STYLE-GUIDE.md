# Nigel's Kitchen Table — Hungarian translation style guide

## Core rule

English remains the unchanged default site. Hungarian is a separate, optional
version under `/hu/`. Never overwrite an English page, post, taxonomy term or WP
Recipe Maker recipe.

## Voice

Use natural, warm Hungarian and informal second person singular (`tegeződés`).
The tone should feel knowledgeable and welcoming rather than promotional or
literal. Keep **Nigel's Kitchen Table** as the brand name; translate surrounding
editorial wording naturally.

## Recipe accuracy

- Preserve every quantity, unit, temperature, timing, yield and ingredient order.
- Do not convert metric and imperial measurements during translation.
- Preserve Gutenberg block comments, HTML, shortcodes and WP Recipe Maker links.
- Ingredient and method changes require a separate editorial decision; they are
  not translation work.
- Use `ek.` for tablespoon and `tk.` for teaspoon only when the surrounding
  recipe format already uses abbreviations consistently.
- Keep food-safety, allergy and storage advice equivalent to the English source.

## Preferred culinary terms

| English | Hungarian |
|---|---|
| baking powder | sütőpor |
| bicarbonate of soda | szódabikarbóna |
| caster sugar | apró szemű kristálycukor |
| icing sugar | porcukor |
| plain flour | finomliszt |
| self-raising flour | sütőporos liszt |
| cornflour | kukoricakeményítő |
| double cream | habtejszín |
| aubergine | padlizsán |
| courgette | cukkini |

Use the glossary as a consistency guide, not as permission to alter quantities
or substitute ingredients.

## Titles and SEO

Translate meaning, not word order. Hungarian titles should sound natural in
search results and accurately describe the finished dish. Write a separate
Hungarian slug and Yoast title/description. Do not translate the domain name.

Examples:

- `Chocolate Fudge Cake` → `Csokoládés fudge torta`
- `Brioche Burger Buns` → `Briós hamburgerzsemle`
- `Lemon Crinkle Cookies` → `Citromos pöfeteg keksz`

## Internal links

Link to the Hungarian version only when that destination has been reviewed and
published. Until then, keep the item as a draft. The public language selector
must not offer Hungarian on a page without a published Hungarian counterpart.

## Review order

1. Homepage, Recipes, Collections, Kitchen Notes, About Nigel and Contact.
2. Categories, recipe collections and navigation menus.
3. The most visited recipes in small batches.
4. Remaining recipes and Kitchen Notes.
5. Privacy, affiliate and legal wording with human review.

## Approval states

- `not_started`: blank Hungarian target fields.
- `translated`: first complete Hungarian draft.
- `reviewed`: language and recipe structure checked.
- `approved`: ready for WordPress draft creation and final on-site QA.

Hungarian content must remain unpublished until the article, its separate WP
Recipe Maker recipe, links, metadata and mobile layout all pass review.
